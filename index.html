Who I am

Hi! I’m Sandra De Guzman, 27. I’m taking a Bachelor of Science in Computer Science at Sorsogon State University – Bulan Campus.

To me, Computer Science is not just about code, software, or technology. It’s about growing: learning what I’m capable of, being brave enough to face challenges, and slowly building the future I want for myself.

I’ve learned that success doesn’t happen overnight. It takes dedication, patience, and the courage to keep going, even when things get hard.

Being a student taught me that patience can open doors that knowledge alone can’t. And my time as a Supply Office Intern taught me how to be responsible, adaptable, and how to work with different kinds of people.

Those experiences didn’t just make me a better student, they made me a better person. They taught me that real growth comes from both my wins and my mistakes.

I know I still have a lot to learn. I don’t have all the skills yet, and I don’t have all the answers. But I have the willingness to learn, the drive to work hard, and the openness to grow with every opportunity.

Just like the code I write, I’m still a work in progress, and I’m proud of every step that brings me closer to who I want to be.
